📚 TutorRx – AI-powered Flashcard & Exam Study App

TutorRx is a smart flashcard and practice exam app built with React Native, Node.js, Firebase, and OpenAI. It empowers students, professionals, and lifelong learners to master any subject with AI-driven study tools and real-time collaboration.

  • 🧩 Flashcard Creator – Reinforce concepts in any subject.
  • 🧪 Practice Exam Generator – Simulate real test environments.
  • 🧠 Smart Study Tools – Use AI & spaced repetition to boost retention.
  • 🤝 Deck Sharing – Collaborate with classmates or study groups.
  • ☁️ Cloud Sync – Access study materials anytime, anywhere.
