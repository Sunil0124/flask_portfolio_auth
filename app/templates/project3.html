🍽️ Food Hall Café – Responsive Café Website

Food Hall Café is a modern, responsive café website built using React.js (CRA). This single-page application provides visitors with an elegant and informative overview of a café's offerings and experience.

  • 🏠 Home Section – Engaging banner and welcoming intro.
  • 🍽️ Menu Section – Categorized menu with pricing (e.g., Americano $5.50).
  • 🧑‍🍳 Catering Section – Event catering details with photos.
  • ☕ About Us – Storytelling to enhance brand identity.
  • 📞 Contact Us – Direct communication via contact section.
